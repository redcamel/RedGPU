# Environment

Covers how to configure realistic environmental elements in 3D space, including backgrounds, ambient lighting,
atmospheric scattering, and wind.

## Learning Topics

1. **[SkyBox](./skybox.md)**: Filling the scene background with 360-degree cubemap textures for an infinite sense of
   space
2. **[IBL](./ibl.md)**: Analyzing surrounding environment images to apply realistic reflections and global lighting
3. **[SkyAtmosphere](./skyatmosphere.md)**: Simulating dynamic skies and sunsets through real-time physically-based
   atmospheric scattering
4. **[WindManager](./wind.md)**: Controlling global wind direction and speed to drive natural motion across foliage and
   water
